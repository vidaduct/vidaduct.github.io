Thesis

Launch DUCT paired with VIDAx. As the system develops, add vetted DUCT/token liquidity pairs. Protocol-owned LP fees are transparently reinvested into additional DUCT liquidity and VIDAx acquisition.

Flow

  1. Begin with a DUCT/VIDAx pool.
  2. Route protocol-owned LP fees back into the liquidity network.
  3. Use published rules to evaluate new DUCT/token pools.
  4. Progressively lock qualifying LP positions.
  5. Allow alternate paths to be arbitraged when prices diverge.

Locking an LP position restricts that position; it does not make the underlying VIDAx permanently locked. VIDAx remains tradable through available markets.

Pool expansion rules

  • New pairs must pass published liquidity, integrity, and concentration checks.
  • Pool additions and protocol-owned positions must be disclosed before or when activated.
  • Qualifying protocol-owned LP positions may be progressively locked under disclosed terms.
  • No token or pool is guaranteed inclusion, volume, yield, or price performance.

Transparent metrics to publish

  • Active pool addresses and paired assets
  • Protocol-owned liquidity positions and lock status
  • Fees collected and reinvestment transactions
  • VIDAx acquired by the protocol
  • Liquidity depth, volume, and routing activity by pool
  • Pool additions, removals, and rule changes

Risks

Crypto assets and liquidity pools are high risk. Risks include smart-contract failure, impermanent loss, low liquidity, market manipulation, token concentration, routing failure, regulatory change, and total loss. Arbitrage may not occur, and protocol mechanics do not guarantee demand, volume, yield, liquidity, or price appreciation.
